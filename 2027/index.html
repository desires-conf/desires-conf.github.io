About

The DESIRES conference brings together researchers and practitioners on search and retrieval systems from both academia and industry to discuss the latest innovative and visionary ideas in the field. DESIRES is the place to gather community feedback on ideas and prototypes before you formally evaluate them.

For DESIRES, you thus submit the paper after the conference. The conference program is based on submitted abstracts and focuses on discussions and feedback. Authors of these abstracts are invited after the conference to submit a full paper, to be published in the open access Information Retrieval Research Journal (IRRJ).

Call for Contributions

DESIRES welcomes contributions that demonstrate novel ideas and approaches in search and information retrieval. Contributions must showcase what they propose, for instance through (imagined) example interactions, mock-ups, or a prototype. Contributions from outside computer science or academia are especially welcome.

Relevant topics for DESIRES include, but are not limited to:

  • Novel metrics for relevance, trust, social impact, etc. of search engine use
  • New methods for interacting with information in search
  • Query and document interpretation and augmentation
  • Innovative data collections and search interactions datasets
  • Underexplored search settings and information needs
  • Adaptations of search systems for specific target audiences
  • Adaptations of search systems for specific modalities or languages
  • New search engine interfaces or interface components
  • Simulating the search behavior of specific persona or populations
  • Methods to reduce biases in search or reveal biases to users
  • Countering misinformation and manipulation in search results
  • Red teaming to reveal vulnerabilities of search engines

Prepare and submit your abstract according to the submission guidelines of IRRJ. Note the section on Proceedings of DESIRES 2027, especially that abstracts are expected to be not more than 5 pages plus references. Accepted abstracts will be made available to conference attendees, but not published. Authors are invited after the conference to extend their abstract to a full-size IRRJ paper by adding details, ideas, experiments, or other material.
